# Funding and contributor acknowledgements

This catalogue separates formal dataset creators from the wider community that enabled Ngulia ringing and data collection. It is an acknowledgement record, not an authorship list. The creators used in citation and deposit metadata are maintained in [`config/publication/dataset_metadata.yml`](../config/publication/dataset_metadata.yml). The names below come from Ngulia annual reports held in `data/02_reference/reports/` (1998 and 2004–2024) and the project's long-term acknowledgement record.

## Funders and financial support

- **The Wetland Trust** — the principal recurring funder explicitly acknowledged across the reports. Its support includes ring stock and financial support for project operations.
- **Bird Committee of Nature Kenya** (also reported historically as the EANHS Bird Committee) — support for Kenyan ringer participation and, in 2023, accommodation for Kenyan ringers.
- **Richard Charles** — recurrent individual financial contributions, explicitly acknowledged from 2014 onward.
- **Overseas ringers' levy and individual ringer contributions** — a recurring participant-financed mechanism that enabled Kenyan ringers to take part; identified as especially important in the 2018 and 2020 reports.
- **British Airways Assisting Conservation** — historical support acknowledged in the 1998 report.
- **Frank M. Chapman Fund, Dr Karl Schulze-Hagen and Deutsche Ornithologen-Gesellschaft (DO-G)** — financial support in earlier years.
- **Wetlands International, CIRAD, FAO and the National Museums of Kenya** — funded three Kenyan participants in the 2006 second session for avian-influenza sampling. This was purpose-specific support, not evidence of general long-term project funding.

## Organisations and people with particular importance

- **Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS)** — permissions, free park entry and operational facilitation throughout the report series. The long-term acknowledgement record names former Kenya Wildlife Conservation and Management Department Director Dr Perez Olindo; senior Tsavo West wardens Ted Goss, Bill Woodley and James Isiche; former KWS directors Dr Richard Leakey, Joe Kioko, Dr David Western and Dr Julius Kipng’etich; and Dr Robert Muasya for more recent facilitation. Other KWS staff named in annual reports include Grace Waiguchu, Stephen Nyaga, Phoebe Manyonge, Titus Mutiso and Jeff Ochieng.
- **Ngulia Safari Lodge and its staff** — long-term hosting, care of equipment and operational support. Named managers or staff include Sarah Tomno, Silas Kiti, Mohammed Issak Sheikh, Fred Odhiambo, Steve Katambani, Stella Wambua, Stanley, and Ronald Simiyu.
- **Swedish Ringing Centre / Stockholm Museum rings** — Palaearctic rings since 1990, recovery notification and technical support. The late **Roland Staav**, and more recently **Thord Fransson**, **Lina Jonsson** and **Tuomo Kolehmainen**, are acknowledged for prompt reporting of recoveries and controls.
- **David J. Pearson and Graeme C. Backhurst** — founding and long-term scientific leadership, reporting and historical data creation. The 2018 report notes Backhurst's presence at the first Ngulia autumn ringing day in 1969 and his long-running report production.
- **Colin Jackson** — long-term project leadership, Kenyan ringer training and historical data stewardship.
- **Martin Cade** — project coordination, field work, reporting and photography in recent reports.
- **Anna Forbes-Watson** — a long-term contributor from 1970, recognised in the 2006 report for extraction and scribing; included in memoriam.
- **Edwin Gichohi and Monicah Njambi** — central event administration in 2020.
